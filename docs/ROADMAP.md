## Core Atlas invariants

1. Atlas is model-independent.
2. External AI models are optional tools, never mandatory runtime dependencies.
3. Deterministic-first operation.
4. Human approval for governed changes.
5. Governed execution.
6. Sandbox verification.
7. Fail-closed behavior.
8. Evidence-driven evolution.
9. No speculative mandatory capabilities.
10. Atlas must eventually be capable of developing itself without requiring an
    external AI coding agent or external LLM.
11. Atlas may eventually contain internal learned components if evidence
    demonstrates that they are necessary; this does not violate model
    independence as long as Atlas is not dependent on a specific external
    provider/model.
12. External models may be used during research/development, but they must not
    become permanent architectural dependencies.
